Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart EMI Master ("the App") handles information when you use our Android application.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. Smart EMI Master is designed to provide EMI calculations and related financial calculation features in a simple and convenient way.

1. Information We Collect

Smart EMI Master does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, password, or other personal information to use the core features of the App.

Information that you enter into the App, such as loan amount, interest rate, tenure, EMI values, and other calculation inputs, is used to provide the requested calculations.

2. Financial Information

The App may allow you to enter financial or loan-related information for calculation purposes. This information is used to perform calculations within the App.

We do not use the information you enter into the calculator to provide financial services, approve loans, or make credit decisions.

3. Data Storage

Smart EMI Master is designed to operate primarily on your device. Information entered into the App may be stored locally on your device where required for the App's functionality.

We do not operate a user account system for the App and do not require you to upload your calculator information to our servers.

Advertising providers may collect or use certain information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, IP address, and information about interaction with advertisements, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

These technologies may be used to provide, measure, and improve advertisements and to help prevent advertising fraud.
